# Operator guide: before a paid partnership discussion

The public partners page describes proposed collaborations and timestamped ledger metrics only. It makes no audience or revenue claims. Keep prospect lists, terms, private contact details and any future evidence dossier outside Git with restricted access.

Before pitching paid sponsorship, collect actual evidence appropriate to the proposal, with a date range and source: public recap views or visits (only after a separately approved measurement decision), consented event attendance, agreed placement and duration, and verified deliverables. Distinguish repeat visits from unique visitors; display labels from people; entries from gatherings; ledger quantities from reach. State the sample size, limitations and permission to use each figure. Do not backfill invented historical traffic.

Agree in advance on responsible-use expectations, public naming/image permission, editorial review, how support is disclosed, cancellation and removal, and what happens to copies already shared. Photos, tracking, email campaigns, contracts and paid services require separate decisions. The current release sends no outreach and adds no analytics or pricing.
